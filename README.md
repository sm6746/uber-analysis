# 🚗 Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard** is an end-to-end data analytics and visualization project designed to analyze ride booking data and extract actionable business insights. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **ride volume, vehicle performance, cancellations, ratings, and booking success trends** to support data-driven decision-making.

This project demonstrates strong skills in **data cleaning, KPI design, statistical analysis, and interactive dashboard development**, closely aligned with real-world analytics use cases in mobility and transportation domains.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze large-scale ride booking data to uncover trends and operational patterns
* Evaluate performance across different **vehicle types**
* Identify key reasons behind **ride cancellations and incomplete bookings**
* Monitor **customer and driver ratings** to assess service quality
* Provide stakeholders with **interactive dashboards** for quick decision-making

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description

* **Total Records:** 70,000+ ride bookings
* **Time Period:** January 2024 – December 2024
* **Data Type:** Structured transactional ride data

### Key Attributes

* Booking ID
* Booking Status (Success, Cancelled, Incomplete)
* Vehicle Type (Prime Sedan, Prime SUV, Mini, Auto, Bike, e-Bike)
* Ride Date & Month
* Cancellation Reason (Driver / Customer)
* Customer & Driver Ratings

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

## 📊 Dashboard Structure & Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Overall Dashboard

**Purpose:** High-level operational overview

**Metrics & Visuals:**

* Total bookings count
* Booking status breakdown (Success, Cancelled, Incomplete)
* Monthly ride volume trend

**Insights:**

* Identified seasonal demand fluctuations
* Highlighted months with peak and low ride volumes

---

### 2️⃣ Vehicle Type Analysis

**Purpose:** Compare performance across vehicle categories

**Metrics & Visuals:**

* Total bookings by vehicle type
* Success rate distribution
* Cancellation and incomplete rates by vehicle type

**Insights:**

* Prime Sedan and Mini show balanced demand and success rates
* Auto and Bike categories show higher cancellation sensitivity

---

### 3️⃣ Cancellation Analysis

**Purpose:** Understand why rides fail

**Metrics & Visuals:**

* Cancellations by month
* Customer vs driver cancellation comparison
* Cancellation reasons breakdown

**Insights:**

* Customer-side issues (change of plans, wrong address) are a major driver
* Driver-side cancellations spike during peak-demand months

---

### 4️⃣ Ratings Analysis

**Purpose:** Measure service quality and customer satisfaction

**Metrics & Visuals:**

* Average driver ratings by vehicle type
* Ratings distribution trends

**Insights:**

* Premium vehicle categories maintain consistently higher ratings
* Slight rating drop observed during high-demand periods
